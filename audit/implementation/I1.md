## 1. Source and interpretation contract

The checked sources are thesis Sections 5.2-5.4, Theorem 5.4 and Figs. 5.2-5.6; ACC equations (20)-(38), Theorem 2 and Figs. 2-3; and the legacy `main.m`, `test3_*`, `test5_*`, and helpers. The relevant PDF pages were both text-extracted and visually inspected.

The payoff order is

`[J_1^1,J_2^1,J_1^2,J_2^2] = [J11,J12,J21,J22]`,

and only `J_1^i` is modified. For `Q_i=0`, the implemented incentive is

`tilde J_1^i(x) = zeta_i sigma [U(x)-U(x0)] - omega_i[J_2^i(x)-J_2^i(x0)] + J_1^i(x0)`.

Consequently `p^i(x0)=0` exactly and the complete aggregate identity is

`p^1(x)+p^2(x) = sigma[U(x)-U(x0)] - [J_sum^omega(x)-J_sum^omega(x0)]`,

where

`J_sum^omega = J_1^1 + omega_1 J_2^1 + J_1^2 + omega_2 J_2^2`.

This identity is used in every budget calculation. In particular, the supplementary legacy `test5_3.m` expression omitted the two omega-weighted terms and is not reused as the budget definition.

Necessary feasibility statements and sufficient theorem statements remain separate:

- ACC (20) is a necessary inequality for positive `lambda_i` if a trajectory reaches the target. Its failure for a specified initial state/target rules out that specified positive-weight construction, not every incentive design.
- Theorem 5.4 / ACC Theorem 2 gives the stated stability, weak-improvement, no-trap, and budget guarantees when all listed hypotheses, including invariant-set containment, hold.
- Failure to verify the stronger `D(tilde U,x0) subset D_bud` certificate is not a proof of general infeasibility. Nonzero `Q` can change the geometry.

## 3. Social Pareto set, Nash set, initial state, and target

`I1_design_geometry.png` keeps the centralized social Pareto set and the decentralized Nash set distinct:

- the social Pareto samples solve weighted maximization of all four original payoffs over nonnegative weights;
- the Nash set is the intersection of the two agents' between-BR strips, equivalently the states where each pair of own-gradients has nonpositive product;
- `U`, `J_sum^omega`, and the budget domain are all anchored at the same `x0`;
- the target is the maximizer of `U`, not a finite-time terminal state and not the entire Nash set.

The INC-0 finite-time incentivized endpoint at `t=8` is `(12.8716797802,-9.0518098735)`, at distance `0.0151764070` from the target. It is therefore labeled an endpoint near the target, never as the target itself.

## 4. Omega mechanism: controlled comparison and supplement

For the same INC-0 game, `eta`, `x0`, `sigma`, `zeta`, and the target are fixed while comparing `omega=(0,0)` with `omega=(.25,.25)`:

- at zero omega, the two incentivized `BR_1^i` lines pass through the target, placing it at the corresponding Nash-set corner;
- at positive omega, the stationarity relation applies to `tilde J_1^i + omega_i J_2^i`; the target lies inside the vector-payoff Nash set rather than on both modified first-objective BR lines.

This is the required controlled omega comparison. The separate INC-omega game then shows the mechanism with larger positive weights and the corrected budget, without pretending that its difference from INC-0 is caused by omega alone.

## 5. Sigma and the budget boundary

For the checked thesis legacy game used by `Characterization_of_D_{bud}(sigma)/main.m`, the budget-boundary quadratic Hessian is

`H_bud(sigma) = sigma A_U - A_(J_sum^omega)`.

The determinant roots are `sigma_1=0.4096591512` and `sigma_2=0.5265853903`. The three retained regimes are:

The logical point is parameter-independent: this difference set lies in `D_bud(sigma)^c` for every positive sigma.

## 6. Before/after behavior, payoffs, and transfer

All dynamics use nearest-BR selection with objective 1 on a distance tie and zero motion when an agent's two own-gradients have opposite signs. Curvature scaling makes this consistent with the corresponding legacy scaled-gradient choice for these runs.

### INC-0

- The original trajectory ends near `(8.571444,-4.285751)`; the incentivized trajectory ends near the target.
- Original-system original-payoff changes at `t=8` are `(-3149.925133,34.602613,2065.140708,25.122750)`.
- On the incentivized path, original-payoff changes are `(-1613.393845,58.935415,1728.785517,62.369652)`.
- Modified-payoff changes on that path are `(14.398362,58.935415,33.596178,62.369652)`.
- Aggregate transfer is checked at every output time, not just the endpoint: sampled maximum `0`, minimum `-186.830181`, endpoint `-67.397132`.

The omega-zero design is a boundary case. It provides exact anchoring/budget identities and a successful numerical weak-improvement/no-trap observation, but the positive-`lambda` lemma is not invoked with `lambda=0`.

### INC-omega

- Modified branch behavior moves from `(21,-35)` to `(21,-16.513043)` over `t in [0,5]`; the finite endpoint remains `1.373678` from the target.
- Correct aggregate transfer has sampled maximum `0`, minimum and endpoint `-12874.075388`.
- The old omitted-omega expression gives `-15031.101323` at the endpoint. The discrepancy is substantive and directly demonstrates why `omega_1 Delta J_2^1 + omega_2 Delta J_2^2` must be retained.
- The weighted combinations `tilde J_1^i + omega_i J_2^i` are displayed separately from the four component payoffs.

The unincentivizable `J_2^i` own curvatures are negative and the `(2,2)` branch determinant is `7>0`, satisfying the Assumption 6 / Assumption 1 algebraic part. The remaining three branch determinants are also positive.

What is **not** claimed:

- No complete symbolic construction of the smallest invariant set `N_x0(tilde J)` was obtained for either game.
- A dense independent polar grid found positive budget values inside the larger `D(U,x0)` set (about `1233.94` for INC-0 and `70.18` for INC-omega). Thus the stronger sufficient inclusion `D(tilde U,x0) subset D_bud` is not available for these `Q=0` parameters.
- This failure rejects that stronger certificate only. It does not imply that the observed trajectory leaves the budget domain, that the theorem is false, or that incentive design is generally impossible. If all theorem hypotheses, including invariant-set containment, are established for a design, the theorem supplies its stated guarantees.
