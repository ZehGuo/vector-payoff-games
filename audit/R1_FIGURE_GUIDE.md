# R1 figure guide

This guide separates the complete research repository from a possible website
narrative. It does not authorize publication and does not replace the detailed
task reports or `FIGURE_MANIFEST.md`.

## Suggested review order

1. `T1_production_pollution.png` — application entry: multiobjective preferences
   expand a point equilibrium into a weighted Nash image.
2. `T1_cube_face_correspondence.png` and
   `T1_prism_face_correspondence.png` — weights, faces, edges, and vertices map
   together; use as one paired topology group.
3. `P1_three_payoff_properties.png` and `P1_weak_pareto_trap.png` — improvement
   notions differ, and local weak improvement does not rule out a two-time trap.
4. `X1_domain_map.png` and `X1_trajectories_lyapunov.png` — the coordinate map
   simplifies domains, but a pointwise image is not the independently integrated
   transformed trajectory.
5. `I1_design_geometry.png`, `I1_sigma_budget.png`, and
   `I1_before_after.png` — design target, omega, sigma, behavior, payoffs, and
   complete aggregate transfer form one comparison group.
6. `S1_five_case_overview.png` — compact appendix candidate. Pair its caption
   with the branch/eigenvector evidence from `S1_eigenvectors_transitions.png`.

## Caption guards

- Say “weighted Nash image” for T1; do not say “social Pareto set”.
- Say “new construction” for S1, S2, and the first three P1 games; do not say
  “exact original-figure reproduction”.
- Say “finite-grid witness” for the trap's weak-at-every-time check.
- Say “noncompact by Proposition 3.1/mixed determinant signs” for S2; do not use
  the finite plot window as proof.
- Keep `x(t)`, pointwise `eta(x(t))`, and independently integrated `z(t)` distinct.
- Say the I1 theorem guarantee is conditional; the complete invariant-set
  containment has not been proved for the displayed `Q=0` parameters.
- The unstable S1 case is genuinely unstable under its active-cone witness. A
  coordinate transformation does not erase that direction.
