# Provenance and limitations

This repository reconstructs a research program from papers, retained legacy
code, and newly authorized implementations. Provenance is attached to each
claim and figure so that visual agreement, executable agreement, and theorem
identity are not confused.

## Evidence hierarchy

| Level | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Paper/theorem identity | A result under the paper's stated hypotheses. | That a finite grid independently proves the theorem. |
| Static source or exact algebra | Formula, branch, determinant, eigenstructure, or identity derived from recorded inputs. | That MATLAB ran or that a source figure was recovered pixel-for-pixel. |
| Independent numerical implementation | Agreement from a separately written calculation at its stated discretization/tolerance. | MATLAB provenance, continuous-time proof, or exact source-figure reproduction. |
| Clean MATLAB execution | The declared MATLAB entry generated the recorded artifacts in a new output directory. | A theorem proof or exact reproduction unless separately established. |
| Finite sampling | A property observed on the recorded grid or trajectory. | A continuous-time or global statement. |
| Schematic | A visual explanation of a mathematical possibility. | A sourced parameter set or numerical result. |

An **independent renderer** can be useful for comparison, but its output must
not be called MATLAB output. The current tracked P1 public PNGs were regenerated
by MATLAB in RR-03; the former independent renderings remain historical audit
context only.

## Important scientific boundaries

- `J_j^i` always means objective `j` of agent `i`.
- Weighting in T1 applies jointly to `M` and `b`.
- A vector-payoff Nash set is not a centralized social Pareto set.
- S1/S2 identities come from branch structure and theorem hypotheses, not
  trajectory endpoints or filenames.
- P1 separates own-direction, externality, and total payoff rate; the first
  three games and the legacy trap have different provenance.
- X1 never substitutes a pointwise map or interpolation for the independently
  integrated transformed trajectory.
- I1 retains both omega-weighted terms in the budget identity and does not
  claim the theorem's full guarantee without invariant-set containment.

## Known source discrepancies retained

- For X1, direct evaluation at the recorded initial state gives
  `eta(x0)=(-2.5,-13.25)`, while the paper text prints `(-2.5,-13.5)`.
- For the A/B segment in the X1 source figure, both implementations give a
  decrease in the displayed image norm; the nearby printed inequality points
  in the opposite direction.
- The surviving P1 legacy arrays and the thesis Fig. 4.3 page do not identify a
  unique original generator for all three source panels. The public comparison
  family is therefore labeled new rather than exact reproduction.

These are narrow provenance records, not claims that the associated global
research conclusions are invalid.

## Public clone versus local research workspace

All public links in `README.md` and `docs/` target tracked files. Three local
directories are intentionally ignored:

- `source_material/`: local paper PDFs and extracted source material;
- `reference/`: legacy archives and previous prototypes;
- `results/`: generated MATLAB runs, including local integration-review packs.

A new clone does not contain those directories. The six public experiment
entries are self-contained and write new outputs below `results/public/` by
default. No public reproduction command depends on a local PDF, a legacy
archive, or a previous result bundle.
